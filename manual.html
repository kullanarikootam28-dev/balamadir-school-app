How to use the Balamandir School App

One app, three logins. What you can see and do depends on whether the school office signs you in as an Admin, a Teacher, or a Student (parents share the student's login). This page walks through every screen in plain language, plus the exact spreadsheet format for bulk data uploads.

Admin Teacher Student & Parent

Signing in

everyone
01

First sign-in

Your school office creates your account and gives you an email and a starting password.

  1. Open the app, enter your email and password.
  2. Tap Sign In.
You'll stay signed in after this — the app doesn't ask again each time you open it. You only see this screen again if you tap Sign out.
02

Forgot your password?

After 3 unsuccessful sign-in attempts, two extra links appear under the Sign In button.

  1. Tap Forgot password?
  2. Enter your email and tap Send Reset Link.
  3. Open the email on your phone and tap the link inside it.
  4. Choose a new password and save it.
03

Sign in without typing a password

A one-tap email link, useful if you've forgotten your password but just want to get in.

  1. Tap Sign in with magic link instead.
  2. Enter your email and tap Send Magic Link.
  3. Open the email on your phone and tap the link — you're signed in.
04

Change your password anytime

Available to every role from the key icon at the top of every dashboard.

  1. Tap the key icon next to Sign out.
  2. Type a new password twice and tap Save Password.

Admin panel

runs the whole school's data
01

User Management

Create and manage every teacher, student, and admin account. Search finds one person out of hundreds instantly.

  • Tap Register, fill in name / email / password / role, tap Create.
  • Tap anyone's row to open their full profile — photo, mobile number, class.
  • Use the pencil and bin icons to edit or remove an account.
  • Type in the search box to filter by name or email.
02

Class Configuration

Four tabs that set up how the school's classes are structured.

  • Classes — create a grade + section, assign a homeroom teacher.
  • Subjects — add subject names taught across the school.
  • Assign Teachers — link a teacher to a specific class + subject.
  • Roster — enroll students into a class (searchable list, so it stays fast even with hundreds of students).
03

Timetable Setup

Builds the weekly period grid every class and student sees.

  1. Pick a class.
  2. Fill in each period's subject, teacher, and start/end time on the grid.
04

Global Broadcasts

School-wide notices — every student and teacher sees these in their Alerts.

  1. Tap Broadcast.
  2. Write a title and message.
  3. Optionally tap Attach photo/video.
  4. Tap Post.
05

Spreadsheet Import

Add many classes, timetable periods, or students at once from a spreadsheet instead of typing each one in.

  1. Open the Classes, Timetable, or Students tab.
  2. Tap Download sample sheet to get a correctly-formatted starting file.
  3. Fill it in and save it as .csv or .xlsx.
  4. Tap Pick .xlsx or .csv file and select it.
  5. Check the column mapping and the preview table look right.
  6. Tap Import — any row with a problem is listed with an explanation, and every other row still goes through.
See Spreadsheet formats below for the exact columns each sheet needs.
06

Fee Management

Track what each student owes, mark it paid, and hand over a receipt.

  • Payments tab — add a fee record, mark it paid, filter by status.
  • Tap the share icon on a paid record to generate a PDF receipt and send it — the phone's share sheet includes WhatsApp, so this is how receipts reach a parent today.
  • Reports tab — total collected vs. pending, and a full pending list.
07

Attendance Reports

Charts of attendance across the whole school or one class.

Pick a class (or "All classes") and switch between Daily, Monthly, and Annual views.

08

Marks Reports

Class performance, subject by subject.

Pick a class to see the average score per subject as a bar chart. Pick one subject on top of that to see every student's score in it.

09

Rankings

Who's performing best — visible to admins only.

Students and Teachers tabs, each ranked by average grade, with a trend line for the top performers over time.

10

Complaint Reports

Reads what's been submitted through the student Complaint Box.

Complaints carry no name or student ID — the list is intentionally anonymous, with nothing to trace a message back to whoever sent it.

Teacher panel

only shows your assigned classes
01

Attendance Registry

Mark daily attendance for a class you teach.

  1. Pick your class and the date.
  2. Mark each student Present / Absent / Late / Excused — or tap Mark all present and adjust the few exceptions.
  3. Tap Save Attendance.

Switch to the History tab to see everything already recorded for that class.

02

Gradebook Input

Enter exam or test scores for a class and subject you're assigned to.

  1. Pick the class + subject.
  2. Enter each student's score in the grid.
  3. Tap Save Grades.
03

Class Noticeboard

Post a notice to just one of your classes — not the whole school.

Pick the class, tap Notice, write it, optionally attach a photo or video, and post. Students in that class see it in their Alerts.

04

Messaging Portal

Direct chat with the parents of students in your classes.

Tap a name to open the conversation, type, and send — messages arrive instantly on both ends.

05

My Students

One photo roster of every student across all the classes you teach.

  • Tap any student to open their full profile.
  • If their mobile number is on file, a Message on WhatsApp button opens a chat with them directly — no need to save the number to your phone first.

Student & parent panel

one shared login per student
01

Profile Overview

Your name, roll number, and class, at a glance.

02

Attendance Calendar

A month view — green for present, red for absent — plus your overall attendance percentage against the school's requirement.

03

Academic Report Card

Every recorded score, grouped by subject, with a radar chart comparing subjects at a glance.

04

Daily Timetable

Today's periods — subject, time, and teacher for each.

05

Alerts Dashboard

Every notice that applies to you — school-wide broadcasts and your class's own noticeboard — newest first.

06

Messages

Direct chat with your class teacher.

07

Fee Payments

See what's due, what's overdue, and what's already been paid.

08

Complaint Box

Send a message straight to the school office.

It's genuinely anonymous — your name is never attached, not even for the admin reading it.

Spreadsheet formats

for Spreadsheet Import

Each sheet needs one header row followed by one row per record, saved as .csv or .xlsx. Your column headings don't have to match these names exactly — Spreadsheet Import tries to auto-detect them, and you can re-map any column by hand before importing. What matters is having the right data in the sheet. Tap Download sample sheet inside the app to get a ready-made file in this exact shape.
